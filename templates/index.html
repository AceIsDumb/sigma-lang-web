what is sigma lang?

sigma lang was made for our final year computing coursework.
it attempts to be a very simple to understand and learn, especially for absolute beginners and people who want to pick up programming, but find it too confusing or difficult.

easy to learn

simple syntax and beginner-friendly, mimicking natural language.

documentation

comprehensive guides and examples to help you get started quickly.

open source

public repository on github, contributions welcome
